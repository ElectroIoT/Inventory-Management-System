# :package: **Inventory Management System** :package:

An **Inventory Management System** designed for tracking products, sales, and customers, with a comprehensive dashboard to manage and analyze key business metrics. This system includes features for managing products, recording sales, and generating detailed reports.

## :sparkles: **Features** :sparkles:

### :clipboard: **Admin Dashboard**
- :dart: **Total Products**: Displays the total number of products in the inventory.
- :bar_chart: **Total Sales**: Displays the sum of all sales made.
- :warning: **Low Stock Alerts**: Alerts if any product’s stock is lower than a specified threshold.
- :busts_in_silhouette: **Total Customers**: Displays the total number of customers registered in the system.
- :clipboard: **Navigation**:
  - :package: **Manage Inventory**
  - :gift: **Add New Product**
  - :busts_in_silhouette: **View Customers**
  - :moneybag: **View Sales**
  - :shopping_cart: **Record Sale**
  - :file_folder: **Manage Categories**

### :bar_chart: **Sales Performance Dashboard**
- :chart_with_upwards_trend: **Monthly Sales**: Line chart displaying sales totals over the last 12 months.
- :bar_chart: **Top Selling Products**: Bar chart showing the top 5 selling products by quantity.
- :pie_chart: **Sales by Payment Mode**: Pie chart displaying the breakdown of sales by payment method (Credit, Cash, Online).
- :scroll: **Text-Based Report**:
  - :moneybag: **Total Sales** for the last 12 months.
  - :package: **Top-Selling Products** with quantities.
  - :credit_card: **Sales by Payment Mode**.

### :busts_in_silhouette: **Customer Management**
- :add: **Add New Customer**: Form to add a new customer with name, email, phone number, and address.
- :credit_card: **Update Customer Balance**: Option to update customer balances for **credit**, **cash**, and **PhonePe**.
- :page_facing_up: **View Customer Reports**: Detailed report of a customer’s sales, balance, transactions, and more.

### :moneybag: **Sales and Transactions**
- :memo: **Record Sale**: Allows the admin to select a customer, product, quantity, and payment mode (credit, cash, online).
- :bar_chart: **Sales Report**: View and manage sales records with detailed information about the product, customer, payment mode, and sale date.
- :credit_card: **Credit Payment Option**: If a customer purchases on credit, the payment will be debited from their **net balance**.

### :shopping_cart: **Inventory Management**
- :file_folder: **Manage Inventory**: Add, update, or remove products.
- :label: **Product Categories**: Manage categories for products.
- :warning: **Low Stock Alerts**: Notifications for products with stock less than 5 units.
- :floppy_disk: **Bulk Import/Export**: Option to import/export products using CSV files (future feature).

### :page_facing_up: **Customer Reports**
- :busts_in_silhouette: **Customer Report Page**: Shows detailed information about the customer, including:
  - :memo: **Customer Name, Email, Phone**
  - :credit_card: **Credit Balance, Debit, Cash, PhonePe Balance**
  - :money_with_wings: **Net Balance** (sum of credit, debit, cash, phonepe).
- :bar_chart: **Transaction History**: Shows all transactions (debits and credits) associated with the customer.
- :package: **Sales History**: Lists all sales for the customer.

### :file_folder: **Export/Print Options**
- :floppy_disk: **Export CSV**: Export customer reports and sales data to CSV format.
- :printer: **Print**: Print the customer report or sales report using the browser’s print functionality.

### :shield: **Admin Profile**
- :id_card: **Profile Page**: Admin can view and edit their profile details, including name and email.
- :key: **Change Password**: Admin can change their password for added security.
